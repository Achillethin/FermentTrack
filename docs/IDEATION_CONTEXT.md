# Ideation Context

This project was born from a 10-loop multi-agent ideation sprint that evaluated 8+ side-project ideas for a data scientist interested in food, fermentation, and sports.

## Why FermentTrack Won

| Criterion | Score |
|-----------|-------|
| Fun / passion fit | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| Technical moat (with FermentGraph) | ⭐⭐⭐ |
| Speed to first revenue | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| Solo-dev feasibility | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| Market validation | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ |

### Key Validation Findings

1. **Brewfather** ($8/mo, 200K+ users) proves batch-tracking WTP for beer
2. **Kombucha, koji, cheese, miso** are completely unserved by any dedicated tool
3. The sourdough app market IS crowded (15+ apps) — but multi-ferment is not
4. The product is "reminders + never-forget," NOT "ML predictions" (build value first, add intelligence later)
5. FermentGraph gives a unique moat: no other batch tracker has a compound knowledge graph underneath

### What Was Killed

- ❌ "Fermentation Foundation Model" — too research-heavy for a side project, sim-to-real gap
- ❌ "Predictive Flavor Engine" — KEGG→FlavorDB→perceived flavor is an inferential leap too far
- ❌ "Sourdough-only AI" — market is shockingly crowded (Flourwise, SourdoughGuru, 13+ others)

### The Compound Play

FermentTrack shares structural DNA with the #1 ranked idea (CausalSelf — n=1 causal inference for athletes):
- Both track **biological experiments** over time
- Both need **structured logging + reminders**
- Both benefit from **Bayesian comparison** of interventions/batches
- If one fails, pivot to the other without rebuilding

## Sprint Methodology

- 13 agents dispatched across 10 loops
- Market research, ML landscape analysis, devil's advocate, rubber-duck stress tests
- Deep validations: HACCP market (600K businesses, real but one-time), athlete causal (zero competitors confirmed)
- Cross-pollination, Click Foundation Sprint, product strategy synthesis
