## B. Recipes

### rice_koji_steamed_rice — Rice koji from steamed white rice

- Sources and locators: BCCDC-koji-miso p. 10 states cooked rice is cooled to 35 C, inoculated, and incubated at about 30 C; it gives 27-30 C for protease-directed rice koji, 30 C baseline, 2-5 C above 30 C for sweet miso enzyme profile, never above 40 C, 98% starting humidity, and 42-48 h completion. Jung-koji-fish-sauce-2022 Materials gives rice koji made at 30 C, 80% humidity, about 48 h. Kagawa-miso-manual p. 1 says 15 kg rice becomes 17 kg koji; p. 5 gives 20 g koji starter for 15 kg rice.
- Derivations: temperature median 30 C; lo-hi 27-40 C from BCCDC. Duration median 45 h from 42-48 h. Ingredient normalization: 15 kg rice + derived 2 kg water uptake + 0.020 kg spores = 17.020 kg; rice 15/17.020 = 881.3 g/kg; water 2/17.020 = 117.5 g/kg; spores 0.020/17.020 = 1.2 g/kg.
- One-line method: inoculate cooled steamed rice with commercial koji spores and incubate aerobically with humidity and heat control until white koji growth is complete before dark sporulation.

### barley_koji_single_grain — Barley koji

- Sources and locators: BCCDC-koji-miso p. 5 states starter grown on rice, barley, or wheat is named rice koji or barley koji; pp. 14-15 give general koji control points of 27-40 C, high starting humidity around 98%, ventilation later, and sanitary mixing. BCCDC p. 10 gives 42-48 h completion for koji manufacture.
- Derivations: ingredient mass is a normalized single-substrate bed: Pearl barley 1000 g/kg. Temperature and duration use the same sourced koji envelope as rice koji: 30 C median, 27-40 C range, 45 h median from 42-48 h.
- One-line method: inoculate prepared barley substrate with commercial koji starter and incubate aerobically under koji humidity/ventilation control until koji growth is complete.

### shiro_miso_kagawa_sweet — Short sweet white rice miso

- Sources and locators: Kagawa-miso-manual p. 1 gives sweet/white rice miso maturity of 10 days to 1 month, sweet-miso formula of 17 kg rice koji, 15 kg boiled soybeans, 2 kg seed water, and 2.0-2.4 kg salt, with finished salt 5.5-6.6%. BCCDC-koji-miso p. 10 gives controlled miso fermentation at 25-30 C; p. 14 says typical miso salt is 6-16% and at least 4%; p. 15 gives target aw <=0.85 and mature aw about 0.80.
- Derivations: duration 10 days to 1 month = 240-720 h, median 480 h. Ingredient medians use 2.2 kg salt: total 17+15+2+2.2 = 36.2 kg; rice koji 469.6 g/kg, soybeans 414.4 g/kg, water 55.2 g/kg, salt 60.8 g/kg. Ingredient lo-hi uses salt endpoints 2.0 and 2.4 kg. Salt percent uses the source's finished-miso labels: 5.5-6.6%, median 6.1%.
- One-line method: mix finished rice koji, cooked soybeans, seed water, and low salt into a paste and mature briefly for a sweet white miso.

### red_rice_miso_kagawa_long — Long red rice miso

- Sources and locators: Kagawa-miso-manual p. 1 gives red rice miso maturity of 6 months to 1 year and spicy/red-style formula of 17 kg rice koji, 30 kg boiled soybeans, 1 kg seed water, and 5.8-7.2 kg salt, with finished salt 10.7-13.0%. BCCDC-koji-miso p. 10 gives controlled miso fermentation at 25-30 C; p. 14 gives typical salt 6-16% and at least 4%; p. 15 gives target aw <=0.85 and mature aw about 0.80.
- Derivations: duration 6 months to 1 year = 4320-8760 h, median 6540 h. Ingredient medians use 6.5 kg salt: total 17+30+1+6.5 = 54.5 kg; rice koji 311.9 g/kg, soybeans 550.5 g/kg, water 18.3 g/kg, salt 119.3 g/kg. Ingredient lo-hi uses salt endpoints 5.8 and 7.2 kg. Salt percent uses the source's finished-miso labels: 10.7-13.0%, median 11.9%.
- One-line method: mix rice koji with a soybean-heavy, higher-salt paste and mature for a long red rice miso.

### budu_traditional_anchovy_fish_sauce — Traditional anchovy budu fish sauce

- Sources and locators: Kanjan-budu-2021 Introduction states budu is made from small marine fish, mostly Stolephorus spp., mixed with salt 25-30% w/w and naturally fermented anaerobically for 12-18 months; Materials "Budu processing and sampling" gives an experimental baseline of anchovy 1 kg mixed with 25% sea salt and fermented at room temperature in earthen jars. Codex-fish-sauce section 2.2 says fish sauce is fish mixed with salt and generally fermented not less than 6 months; section 3.4 gives pH 5.0-6.5 typical for traditional product and salt not less than 200 g/L; section 6.4 gives histamine <=40 mg/100 g.
- Derivations: duration 12-18 months = 8760-13140 h, median 10950 h. The article method clarifies 25% sea salt as an addition to 1 kg anchovy, so the 25-30% source range is converted from fish-mass basis to total-batch basis: 25/(100+25)=20.0%, 30/(100+30)=23.1%, median 27.5/(100+27.5)=21.6%. Ingredient medians use fish:salt 100:27.5, giving anchovies 784.3 g/kg and salt 215.7 g/kg.
- One-line method: mix small anchovies with high salt and ferment anaerobically for many months before separating the sauce.

### pacific_sand_lance_rice_koji_fish_sauce — Rice-koji Pacific sand lance fish sauce

- Sources and locators: Jung-koji-fish-sauce-2022 abstract states six fish sauces used rice koji pretreatment for 24 or 72 h, salt contents 10 or 20%, and 10 months fermentation. Materials and Fig. 1 state rice koji is added at 10% of fish paste, incubated at 20 C for 24 or 72 h, then 10% or 20% salt is added, and the sauce is fermented at 18 C for 10 months. The same Materials section states the rice koji used in the study was incubated at 30 C and 80% humidity for about 48 h.
- Derivations: main fermentation 10 months = 7200 h. Temperature is 18 C for main fermentation, with 20 C high endpoint to include the koji pretreatment stage. Salt is converted from source additions of 10-20% of fish-paste mass plus 10% rice koji of fish-paste mass: lo 10/(100+10+10)=8.3%, median 15/(100+10+15)=12.0%, hi 20/(100+10+20)=15.4%. Ingredient medians use fish:rice koji:salt 100:10:15, giving fish 800.0 g/kg, rice koji 80.0 g/kg, and salt 120.0 g/kg.
- One-line method: pre-incubate chopped sand lance with rice koji, add salt, and ferment cold for 10 months.

## D. Envelope check

- **rice_koji_steamed_rice**: profile `koji` is exploratory, typical 30 C, range 28-35 C, horizon 72 h. Recipe 27-40 C and 42-48 h. Conflict: recipe endpoints fall outside profile range (27 C below, 40 C above); BCCDC also says never exceed 40 C, while risk rules flag high aw above 33 C unless certified A. oryzae starter is used.
- **barley_koji_single_grain**: profile `koji` as above. Recipe uses same 27-40 C and 42-48 h envelope. Same temperature-endpoint conflict as rice koji.
- **shiro_miso_kagawa_sweet**: profile `miso` is exploratory, typical 25 C, range 15-30 C, horizon 4320 h. Recipe 25-30 C, 240-720 h, 5.5-6.6% salt. No temperature conflict; duration is much shorter than the profile's long default horizon, as expected for sweet white miso.
- **red_rice_miso_kagawa_long**: profile `miso` as above. Recipe 25-30 C, 4320-8760 h, 10.7-13.0% salt. No temperature conflict; duration spans the profile horizon and extends beyond it.
- **budu_traditional_anchovy_fish_sauce**: profile `garum` is exploratory, typical 30 C, range 20-60 C, horizon 4320 h. Source gives room temperature without a numeric value, so no temperature comparison; duration 8760-13140 h exceeds the profile horizon; salt 20.0-23.1% total-batch is higher than the profile's 12% typical koji-garum default.
- **pacific_sand_lance_rice_koji_fish_sauce**: profile `garum` as above. Recipe main fermentation 18 C, pretreatment 20 C, duration 7200 h, salt 8.3-15.4%. Conflict: 18 C main fermentation is below the profile's 20 C lower bound; duration exceeds the profile horizon.
