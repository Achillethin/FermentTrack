## B. Recipes

### sauerkraut_dry_salted — Dry-salted sauerkraut

- Sources: NCHFP-sauerkraut; Morton-salt; NIST-conversions.
- Facts copied: 25 lb cabbage; 3/4 cup canning or pickling salt; procedure also says 3 Tbsp salt per 5 lb; 70-75 deg F for 3-4 weeks.
- Derivations: 25 lb cabbage x 0.45 kg/lb = 11.25 kg cabbage. Morton 1/4 tsp = 1.5 g, so 1 Tbsp = 18 g. Ingredient-list salt 3/4 cup = 12 Tbsp = 216 g. Procedure salt = 3 Tbsp x 5 cabbage batches = 15 Tbsp = 270 g. Salt percent range = 216/(11250+216) = 1.88% to 270/(11250+270) = 2.34%; median 2.11%. Normalised salt = 18.8-23.4 g/kg; cabbage is the complement. 70-75 deg F = 21.1-23.9 deg C. 3-4 weeks = 504-672 h.
- One-line method: Shred cabbage, dry-salt it, pack until its own brine covers it, and ferment weighted under brine.
- Notes: NCHFP ingredient list and procedure disagree on salt volume; the CSV range deliberately covers both rather than choosing one.

### dill_cucumber_pickles_brined — Brined dill cucumber pickles

- Sources: NCHFP-dill-pickles; Morton-salt; NIST-conversions.
- Facts copied: 4 lb cucumbers; 2 Tbsp dill seed or 4-5 heads dill; 1/2 cup salt; 1/4 cup vinegar; 8 cups water; 70-75 deg F for 3-4 weeks.
- Derivations: cucumbers = 4 lb x 0.45 kg/lb = 1.80 kg. Water = 8 cups x 0.24 L/cup = 1.92 kg. Vinegar = 1/4 cup x 0.24 L/cup = 0.06 kg, treated as water-density for g/kg. Salt = 1/2 cup = 8 Tbsp x 18 g/Tbsp = 144 g. Total normalised mass excluding dill = 3924 g; salt = 3.67%; cucumber 458.7 g/kg, water 489.3 g/kg, salt 36.7 g/kg, vinegar 15.3 g/kg. 70-75 deg F = 21.1-23.9 deg C. 3-4 weeks = 504-672 h.
- One-line method: Pack cucumbers with dill, cover with salty vinegar brine, weight below brine, and ferment at room temperature.
- Notes: Draft because the required dill is source-stated as volume or heads, not mass.

### napa_kimchi_room_temp — Napa-cabbage kimchi

- Sources: Jang2024-kimchi; CSU-kimchi; NCSU-kimchi; NIST-conversions.
- Facts copied: Jang2024 formulation with 84% salted kimchi cabbage, 3% red pepper powder, 3% radish, 0.8% ginger, 2.5% onion, 1.5% salted shrimp, 0.8% glutinous rice paste, 4.4% water, and 2% garlic selected from the reported 0-4% garlic treatments. CSU reports final salt 2-5%, optimum pH 4.2, pH <=4.6 before consuming, and 1-2 days at room temperature; NCSU gives 68 deg F as ideal.
- Derivations: selected 2% garlic treatment sums to 102 parts, so each ingredient share is divided by 102 and multiplied by 1000. Salted cabbage normalises to 823.5 g/kg. CSU final salt 2-5% becomes 20-50 g/kg salt, median 35 g/kg; Napa cabbage is salted-cabbage remainder, 773.5-803.5 g/kg with 788.5 g/kg median. 68 deg F = 20.0 deg C. 1-2 days = 24-48 h.
- One-line method: Use salted napa cabbage with kimchi seasonings, pack tightly with little air, and ferment at room temperature until pH is <=4.6.
- Notes: Uses a peer-reviewed percentage formulation but the room-temperature endpoint comes from CSU/NCSU extension guidance; the peer-reviewed garlic study itself stored samples at 4 deg C.

### carrot_sticks_wet_brined — Wet-brined carrot sticks

- Sources: UMN-fermentation; Morton-salt; NIST-conversions.
- Facts copied: wet brines work for carrot sticks; most vegetables use 1-3 Tbsp salt per 1 quart water; ideal temperature 68-72 deg F; ferment 3-4 weeks; ready at pH <=4.60.
- Derivations: 1 quart water = 0.95 kg. Morton 1 Tbsp salt = 18 g. Brine-only salt range = 18/(950+18) = 1.86% to 54/(950+54) = 5.38%; median with 2 Tbsp = 36/(950+36) = 3.65%. Brine-only normalisation gives water 963.5 g/kg and salt 36.5 g/kg at the median. 68-72 deg F = 20.0-22.2 deg C. 3-4 weeks = 504-672 h.
- One-line method: Cover carrot sticks with a 1-3 Tbsp/quart wet brine, keep submerged, and ferment until acidified.
- Notes: Draft because UMN gives brine strength but not the carrot-to-brine mass needed for total-batch g/kg; CSV ingredient mass is brine-only and explicitly marked.

### black_tea_kombucha_1f — Black-tea kombucha first ferment

- Sources: Cohen2023-kombucha.
- Facts copied: 1 L black tea; 8 g/L loose-leaf black tea; 100 g white granulated sugar; 10% starter tea; 30 deg C; two weeks. Sensory attributes in the same study include sweetness, sourness, vinegar flavor, apple juice/cider, lemon/citrus, and yeast.
- Derivations: 10% starter tea on 1 L is treated as 100 g starter liquid. Total starting mass = 1000 g water + 8 g tea + 100 g sugar + 100 g starter = 1208 g. Normalised per kg: water 827.8 g/kg, black tea leaves 6.6 g/kg, cane sugar 82.8 g/kg, starter liquid 82.8 g/kg. Two weeks = 336 h.
- One-line method: Sweeten black tea, inoculate with mature kombucha starter tea, and ferment aerobically.
- Notes: This is the source's starter-culture tea for its kombucha experiment; its 30 deg C temperature is outside the current kombucha profile range.

### green_tea_kombucha_variant — Green-dominant kombucha first ferment

- Sources: Dartora2023-kombucha.
- Facts copied: total tea 7 g/L; high-green treatment green:black ratio 79.8:20.2; sucrose range 6.81-9.19%; SCOBY weight range 15.03-22.47 g per 500 mL; 25 deg C; 10 days.
- Derivations: high-green tea per liter = 7 x 0.798 = 5.586 g green tea and 7 x 0.202 = 1.414 g black tea. Sucrose = 68.1-91.9 g/L with 80 g/L median. SCOBY = 30.06-44.94 g/L with 37.5 g/L median. Median total = 1000 + 7 + 80 + 37.5 = 1124.5 g. Median normalised values: water 889.3 g/kg, sugar 71.1 g/kg, SCOBY 33.3 g/kg, green tea 5.0 g/kg, black tea 1.3 g/kg. 10 days = 240 h.
- One-line method: Brew a green-dominant tea infusion, sweeten it, add SCOBY, and ferment aerobically at 25 deg C.
- Notes: This is a green-dominant mixed-tea treatment, not a pure green-tea kombucha.

## D. Envelope check

- Profiles read from `profiles.py`:
  - `lacto_ferment`: typical 20 deg C; range 16-24 deg C; horizon 672 h; typical recipe includes 20 g/kg salt.
  - `kombucha`: typical 24 deg C; range 20-28 deg C; horizon 504 h; typical recipe includes 830 g/kg water and 70 g/kg sucrose; starter fraction 0.10.
- `sauerkraut_dry_salted`: 21.1-23.9 deg C is inside `lacto_ferment`; 504-672 h reaches the 672 h horizon; salt 1.88-2.34% straddles the 2% vegetable-ferment warning threshold because the NCHFP salt statements conflict.
- `dill_cucumber_pickles_brined`: 21.1-23.9 deg C is inside `lacto_ferment`; 504-672 h reaches the horizon; salt 3.67% is above the profile's typical 2% but inside the 2-10% safety band.
- `napa_kimchi_room_temp`: 20 deg C is exactly the `lacto_ferment` typical temperature; 24-48 h is much shorter than the 672 h default horizon but sourced for room-temperature kimchi; salt 2-5% is inside vegetable-ferment safety guidance.
- `carrot_sticks_wet_brined`: 20.0-22.2 deg C is inside `lacto_ferment`; 504-672 h reaches the horizon; brine-only salt 1.86-5.38% has a lower edge below the 2% warning and is draft because total-batch salt depends on unknown carrot-to-brine mass.
- `black_tea_kombucha_1f`: 30 deg C is outside the `kombucha` 20-28 deg C profile range; 336 h is inside the 504 h horizon; sugar 82.8 g/kg is above the profile typical 70 g/kg.
- `green_tea_kombucha_variant`: 25 deg C is inside the `kombucha` profile range; 240 h is inside the horizon; sugar 71.1 g/kg matches the profile typical closely.

## E. Unverified leads

- Flavoured kombucha second ferment: searched/opened kombucha literature leads, but no tested source was opened that stated flavouring g/kg, second-ferment temperature, and second-ferment time together. No recipe row added.
- Pure green-tea kombucha with exact starter percentage: Dartora2023 gives a high-green mixed-tea treatment (79.8:20.2 green:black), not a pure green-tea formulation. No pure-green row added.
- Codex CXS 223-2001 kimchi: web/search showed the standard, but direct FAO/mirror fetches returned 403 in this environment, so its numbers were not used as opened evidence.
