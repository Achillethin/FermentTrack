# Aroma B4 — Miso and Garum: Slow Chemistry (HEMF, Furanones, Maltol, Pyrazines, Strecker), Fish Lipids

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Miso and garum get aroma: the yeast-gated HEMF chain, furanones, maltol and pyrazines from the cooked substrates, Strecker aldehydes from the free amino acids, koji carry-over, and fish-lipid oxidation; curation tests 38–51 pinned (both are exploratory types: strict xfails with reasons are expected); the trimethylamine invariant restored to ×2.

**Architecture:** One new template step (T11) and two small sources in `aroma.concentrations`:
- **Strecker** (§ 5.11): free amino acids (engine pool × the § 5.1 shares) → aldehydes at a mass rate (µg per g amino acid per day, Q10), and a slow aldehyde → acid oxidation.
- **HEMF** (§ 5.11, D13): a precursor formed at a zero-order rate that follows the koji amylase activity (normalised to its start) and needs soybean; *Z. rouxii* converts it only below pH 5.6; HEMF decays chemically with a steep Q10.
- **Furaneol** (zero-order, barley), **norfuraneol** (soybean pool taken up by *Z. rouxii*), **maltol** (soybean and barley pools, first-order loss), **pyrazines** (barley-koji pool), **koji carry-over** of 1-octen-3-ol into miso.
- **Fish lipids** (§ 5.8): five zero-order sources per kg of fish with a slow loss.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.11, numpy/scipy, pytest, mypy (`--python-version 3.12`), ruff. No frontend change.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-05-aroma-curation.md` (§ 4.8 miso, § 4.9 garum, D13, § 5.8, § 5.11, § 6.1–6.2, § 7 tests 38–51 and the D5 invariant, § 10.3–10.5). Previous plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-06-aroma-b3.md`.
