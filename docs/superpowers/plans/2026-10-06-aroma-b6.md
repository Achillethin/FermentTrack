# Aroma B6 — Ingredient Pass: Spice, Vegetable, Rice and Flour Precursors; Aroma Ingredients in the Catalogue

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Groundwork for the owner's recipe builder: the ingredient-only compounds that computed zero ("P0 pending") get estimated, wide precursor priors keyed by ingredient, and aroma-relevant ingredients join the catalogue through a migration. The recipe builder itself is not built.

**Architecture:**
- **Data** in `aroma_data.AROMA_INGREDIENTS`, per kg of the logged ingredient: geranial and neral (ginger, lemongrass, lemon), carvone and limonene (caraway, dill, mint), vegetable free amino acids and hydroxycinnamic acids (cabbages, cucumber, radish, carrot, chilies), rice 2-acetyl-1-pyrroline, flour sotolon. Every number is `est. (B6)` with its one-line reasoning (typical essential-oil yield × oil composition, or composition order) and a deliberately wide range; no source was opened for them.
- **Templates:** T10 gains p-coumaric acid → 4-vinylphenol (same enzymes and parameters as ferulic acid). Vegetable free amino acids feed the **chemical Strecker route only** (ruling below); the microbial first-order routes of § 5.1 stay on the engine's pool.
- **Catalogue:** `seed_data.INGREDIENT_SEED_DATA_V4` + migration `0017`, following V3. No FDC nutrient rows: the snapshot needs the FDC API key (`scripts/fetch_fdc_snapshot.py`), so that part is skipped and reported; the new rows show as "no reference data" in composition, as starters and tea already do.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.11, numpy/scipy, SQLAlchemy/Alembic, pytest, mypy (`--python-version 3.12`), ruff. No frontend change.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-05-aroma-curation.md` (§ 3 status column, § 4 † cells, § 5.10, § 6, § 10.2, § 10.6 on the aminotransferase prior, § 11.2). Previous plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-06-aroma-b5.md`. Catalogue pattern: `src/fermenttrack/seed_data.py`, `alembic/versions/0005_split_vague_ingredients.py`, `tests/test_migration_0016.py`.
