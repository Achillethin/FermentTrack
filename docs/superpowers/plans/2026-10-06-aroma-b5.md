# Aroma B5 — One Mass Basis; Kombucha, Kefir and Cheese: Citrate Chain, Tea Terpenes, Milk Pools

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** First make the aroma layer use one mass basis throughout (a deferred minor of B1). Then kombucha, kefir and cheese get aroma: the T4 citrate → α-acetolactate chain, the tea's free and bound terpenes (T9), the milk's carbonyls, fatty acids and lactones, and kombucha's open surface. Curation tests 1–10 and 26–37 pinned.

**Architecture:**
- **Mass basis (ruling):** tracers integrate as amounts per kg of *starting* batch, like the engine's pools (a source made from a flux or an amount is per kg start without conversion). Only second-order chemistry (esterification) reads molar concentrations of the current mass (`/ left`) and converts its rate back (`× left`). Every compound is divided by the mass left (`left`) once, at the end, so odour activity and the API read per kg of what is in the jar. A chain carries per-current values into the next mix.
- **T4 citrate** (§ 5.4, D3): the milk's citrate is taken up by citrate-active *Lactococcus* (a per-member active share) and by *Leuconostoc* once hexoses fall below 1.8 g/kg; half of the C4 share of citrate-derived pyruvate becomes α-acetolactate, which joins B2's sugar-route pool.
- **Milk** (§ 5.8, § 6.2): carried pools; δ-lactones released from a fat-bound precursor.
- **Tea** (§ 5.9, § 6.2): free terpenes and lipid aldehydes carried in; a bound glycoside pool released by yeast β-glucosidase and acid; per-compound losses.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.11, numpy/scipy, pytest, mypy (`--python-version 3.12`), ruff. No frontend change.
