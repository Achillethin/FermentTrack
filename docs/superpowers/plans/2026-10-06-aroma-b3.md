# Aroma B3 — Vinegar and Koji: the Acetic-Acid-Bacteria Sink, Wine Base, Open Surfaces, Mould Volatiles

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Vinegar and koji get aroma: the acetic acid bacteria (AAB) consume fusel alcohols, acetaldehyde and 2,3-butanediol on top of a wine/cider base; open vessels lose volatiles at their surface; *A. oryzae* makes C8 compounds, methyl ketones and transient acetate esters as its mycelium grows. Curation tests 11–13 and 16–18 pinned.

**Architecture:** Three additions to `aroma.concentrations`, each a template of the curation spec:
- **T12 open surface** (§ 5.12): a per-type surface rate `k_surf` (vinegar, koji bed) added to every tracer's volatility loss as `k_surf × K_aw`.
- **AAB sink** (§ 5.1, § 5.3, § 5.4, D11): first-order oxidation gated by AAB biomass (X/x_max), with the fusel acids and acetoin as products.
- **Mould a-terms** (§ 5.2, § 5.8): sources proportional to the mould's growth (mg per g mycelium made); acetate esters lost to fungal esterase as growth stops.
- **Base pools** (§ 6.2): the wine/cider base's volatiles per kg of a 94 g/kg-ethanol base, scaled by the batch's starting ethanol over 94 g/kg (the spec's rule), shared between the logged base ingredients.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.11, numpy/scipy, pytest, mypy (`--python-version 3.12`), ruff. No frontend change.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-05-aroma-curation.md` (§ 4.2 vinegar, § 4.4 koji, § 5.1–5.4, § 5.8, § 5.12, § 6.2, § 7 tests 11–13 and 16–18, § 10.4 B2 notes). Previous plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-06-aroma-b2.md`.

## Owner decisions (binding, 2026-10-06)

1. Threshold medians = the latest *reliable* determination (§ 3 rule).
2. Richest a-priori palette: every active compound with a threshold counts in the series sums; the tier is a badge and line style only.
3. Every computed compound records its routes (ingredient + precursor, organism, chemistry); the recipe builder itself is not built here.

## Global Constraints

- Non-aroma forecast output stays byte-identical; the existing suite passes untouched.
- Aroma never breaks a forecast, nor taste and nutrition (guards unchanged).
- numpy/scipy only; no new frontend dependencies.
- Wording: "may be noticeable", never "smells like". Spoilage odours are not modelled.
- Units: µg/kg; a-terms mg per g biomass made (`growth:j` in g/kg/h → dC = 1000·a·growth µg/kg/h); k_max 1/d at carrying capacity; tracer rates 1/h.
- New numbers carry their spec tag or `# est. (B3)` / `# derived (B3)` and are written back (§ 10.5, § 11.2).
- Calibrate only by moving a median inside its stated (lo, hi); otherwise `xfail(strict=True, reason=…)` plus a § 10.5 note, split so passing parts stay live.
- Record the timing per forecast (vinegar 60 d, koji 72 h), with and without aroma.

## Review Focus

1. **A closed jar** (lacto-ferment, sourdough) gets no surface loss. Pinned in Task 1 (`test_surface_loss_only_in_open_vessels`).
2. **A diluted wine base** (half wine, half water) halves the base pools through the starting ethanol, not twice (share and ethanol). Pinned in Task 1 (`test_wine_base_scales_with_starting_ethanol`).
3. **A vinegar recipe logged as cider** ("Hard cider", no FDC row, typical-recipe fallback) still gets the base pools. Pinned in Task 1 (same test, parametrised).
4. **Koji without growth** (a mould that cannot grow) makes no C8 compounds. Pinned in Task 2 (`test_no_growth_no_mould_volatiles`).
5. **AAB alone** make the fusel acids only from alcohol that exists (mass balance). Pinned in Task 1 (`test_aab_oxidation_mass_balance`).

def _ratio(v: tuple[At, float], key: str) -> float:
    c, end = v
    start = c(key, 0.0)
    return c(key, end) / start if start > 0.0 else float("inf") * (c(key, end) > 0.0)
